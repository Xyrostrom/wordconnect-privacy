Word Connect — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Word Connect is a word puzzle game published by Shikhar Singh. This page explains exactly what the app stores, why, who else can see it, and how to get rid of it. It covers the Android app with package name com.shikhar.crossword.

What the app stores about you

DataWhyWhere it goes
An account identifier So your progress follows you to a new device. The app signs you in anonymously on first launch; you only get a named account if you choose to sign in with Google. Google Firebase Authentication
Your name and profile picture, if you sign in with Google Shown on your profile, and next to your score on the daily-challenge leaderboard. Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore
Game progress — gems, daily streak, solved levels, words found, achievement counters So the game can continue where you left off. Cloud Firestore
Your country, as a two-letter code To show how many people are playing near you. The app guesses it from your device's region setting, and you can change or clear it in your profile. No precise location is ever requested or stored. Cloud Firestore
A notification token, if you allow notifications To remind you about the daily challenge. The app only asks after you have finished the tutorial and solved a level, and declining changes nothing else. Firebase Cloud Messaging
Your device's advertising identifier Used by Google AdMob to serve the optional rewarded video ads you can watch in exchange for gems. Google AdMob

The app does not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, call logs, messages, or any other app's data. It does not ask for your email address directly — if you sign in with Google, Google tells the app the address attached to the account you picked.

Who else can see it

Your name, profile picture, country and score become visible to other players when you post a daily-challenge result to the leaderboard. Nothing else you store is shown to anyone else.

The app does not sell your data, and does not share it with anyone beyond the Google services listed above, which process it on our behalf:

  • Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Messaging — Firebase privacy and security
  • Google AdMob — how Google uses data from AdMob

Children

Word Connect is suitable for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from a child beyond what is listed above, and a child can play the entire game without ever signing in — anonymous play stores progress against a random identifier with no name attached to it.

Deleting your data

Open Profile → Delete account in the app. This permanently erases your game progress, your profile, and every daily-challenge score published under your name, and signs you out. It cannot be undone.

If you cannot reach the app — for example you have already uninstalled it — email the address below with the name you played under and we will delete the account for you.

Uninstalling the app on its own does not delete anything stored in Firebase.

Retention

Progress is kept for as long as the account exists. If you delete your account it is removed immediately. Anonymous accounts that are never signed into again remain until deleted on request.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who sees it, the date at the top of this page will change and the new version will be published here before the change reaches the app.
